⚠️ Heads Up — New Europe Requirements for Canadians

UK ETA — Already in effect: Since February 2026, all Canadians visiting the UK must obtain an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before flying. It now costs £20 (risen from £10 at launch), takes minutes online, and is valid for 2 years. Children need their own ETA.

EU ETIAS — No confirmed launch date: The EU actually withdrew its "Q4 2026" target in July 2026 — no official launch date currently exists, with 2027 now considered most likely. Not required yet. The fee is expected to be €20 (not the earlier-announced €7) once it does launch.

EU Entry/Exit System (EES) — Now in effect: Since October 2025, European border crossings now scan biometrics (photo + fingerprints) on your first entry. Replaces passport stamping. Expect slightly longer border queues on first entry.

Direct flights from YEG and YYC to Amsterdam, Paris, Frankfurt, Rome, Barcelona, Lisbon and Reykjavik.

🔵 ETIAS Update — Coming Q4 2026

The EU's new pre-travel authorization (ETIAS) no longer has a confirmed launch date — the EU withdrew its "Q4 2026" target in July 2026, with 2027 now considered most likely. It will cost €20 (revised up from the earlier €7 estimate), takes about 10 minutes online, and will be valid for 3 years once live. Not required yet.

The UK ETA is separate from ETIAS and already required. If your trip combines UK + Europe, you need both.

United Kingdom & Ireland

Direct flights from YYC to London and Dublin. NOT in the Schengen Area — separate rules apply.

⚠️ UK ETA — Required Since February 2026

All Canadians must obtain a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before flying to the United Kingdom. This includes England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

  • Cost: £20 per person (approximately $34 CAD) — this rose from £10 at launch, then £16, to £20 as of April 2026
  • Valid for 2 years or until your passport expires
  • Children and infants need their own ETA
  • Apply at least 3 business days before travel

Before Every International Trip — Aunt Tia's Checklist

✓

Passport valid? Most countries require 3–6 months validity beyond your travel dates. Check before booking.

✓

Travel insurance purchased? This is non-negotiable. Medical evacuation from Japan or Thailand can cost $100,000+. A good policy costs $50–$150.

✓

Pre-travel authorizations applied for? UK ETA for London. ETIAS for Europe when it launches (Q4 2026). Check each destination's requirements.

✓

Registered with the Canadian government? Registration of Canadians Abroad is free and lets Ottawa reach you in an emergency.

✓

Medications checked? Some common Canadian medications are restricted or illegal in certain countries (especially Japan, UAE). Check before packing.

✓

Copies of documents? Email yourself scanned copies of your passport, insurance, and tickets. Store in the cloud and leave copies with someone at home.

✓

Credit card travel notifications set? Tell your bank you're travelling or your card may be blocked at the worst possible moment.

✓

Local currency or card sorted? Some destinations are cash-heavy. Know before you land at midnight with no local currency.

✓

Emergency contacts saved? Canadian Embassy numbers for your destination. Your insurance provider's 24-hour emergency line. Someone at home who knows your itinerary.
