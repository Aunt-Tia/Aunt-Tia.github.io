The story behind the deals

Meet Aunt Tia

I've been hunting deals so long, airports feel like home. ✈️

Hi — I'm Aunt Tia.

I'm a Canadian traveller who got tired of paying too much for flights — and even more tired of watching friends and family miss out on incredible trips because they assumed flying was out of their budget.

The truth? Cheap flights out of Canada exist. They're just buried. Airlines price dynamically, deals appear and disappear, and most people don't have the time or patience to check every day. That's where I come in.

Every week I dig through flight data — domestic routes between Canadian cities, and international steals to Europe, the Caribbean, Mexico, Asia, and beyond — and send the best ones straight to your inbox. No fluff, no filler, just real prices on real routes.

Why SkyTia?

I built SkyTia because I couldn't find a Canadian flight deal newsletter that actually felt like it was written for Canadians. Most deal newsletters are US-focused. They'll tell you about a $200 flight from JFK and expect you to get excited about that while you're sitting in Edmonton.

SkyTia is different. Alberta is always included. So is Halifax, Ottawa, and every other city that usually gets forgotten. If you're flying out of Canada, I've got you covered.

What I actually believe

About the affiliate links

SkyTia uses affiliate links — when you book through one of my links, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how I keep the newsletter free. I only share deals I'd genuinely use myself, and I never let affiliate relationships influence which deals I feature. The deal is always the point.

When you click "Book this flight," you're taken to Aviasales — a real, established flight search engine — to complete your booking directly with them or the airline. SkyTia never sees or touches your payment information at any point.

Who's actually behind this

SkyTia is a genuinely independent, one-person project — not a big travel corporation, not a bot, not a content farm. Just someone who got tired of overpaying for flights and decided to build something better for fellow Canadians. Aunt Tia is the voice; a real person is behind the keyboard every single week.
