Flying International from Prince George?

No year-round direct international flights from YXS — but Vancouver's the easiest connection (7-8 flights a day from Prince George) into one of Canada's biggest international hubs, and Calgary and Edmonton both have direct connections to Amsterdam, London, Paris, Frankfurt, Abu Dhabi and more. Heads up: WestJet usually brings back a seasonal non-stop to Puerto Vallarta each winter (past seasons ran Saturdays, roughly November through April) — watch this page for the return date once it's confirmed.
