Get SkyTia on Your Phone 🐾

Takes 10 seconds — no app store, no download. What are you using?

On Android (Chrome):
  1. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) top right
  2. Tap "Install and create shortcut"
  3. Confirm — Aunt Tia's icon lands on your home screen!
← Back
On iPhone (Safari):
  1. First, go to skytia.com (not this page) — type it in your address bar or tap the logo above
  2. Once you're on the homepage, tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) at the bottom
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add" — done!
← Back
On Desktop (Chrome):
  1. Look for the install icon in your address bar (right side, looks like a small screen with a down arrow)
  2. Click it, then click "Install"
  3. SkyTia opens as its own app window!
