Flying from Fort McMurray (YMM) — The Complete 2026 Guide

By Aunt Tia · July 3, 2026 · 5 min read

Fort McMurray Airport serves one of Canada's most important economic regions — the Athabasca oil sands. With over 400,000 passengers annually and a significant workforce on rotation schedules, YMM punches well above its size. Here is everything you need to know about flying from Fort Mac.

"Fort Mac workers know the drill better than anyone — book Sunday night, fly Monday morning, come home Thursday or Friday. The airlines know this too, which is why these routes fill up. Book ahead."

Fort McMurray Airport (YMM) — The Basics

Fort McMurray Airport is located about 8 kilometres northeast of downtown Fort McMurray. The airport has seen significant upgrades in recent years to handle the demands of the oil sands workforce and their families.

  • IATA code: YMM
  • Annual passengers: Approximately 436,000+
  • Airlines serving YMM: WestJet, Air Canada
  • Direct destinations: Calgary, Edmonton, Toronto, Vancouver
  • International flights: None direct — connect through Calgary or Edmonton

Every Direct Flight from Fort McMurray

Fort McMurray has four direct destinations. No international flights operate from YMM — for international travel you connect through Calgary or Edmonton.

✈️ YMM → YYC — Fort McMurray to Calgary

WestJet + Air Canada · Multiple daily flights

Calgary is Fort McMurray's busiest route by a significant margin — approximately 44 flights per week from both WestJet and Air Canada combined. The high frequency reflects both the workforce rotation demand and Calgary's role as a major connecting hub for international travel.

Flight time: Approximately 1 hour 15 minutes

Why fly YMM-YYC: Connect to direct international flights from Calgary — London, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Abu Dhabi and more. Far better international connections than Edmonton.

Typical price range: $200-$450 CAD roundtrip

✈️ YMM → YEG — Fort McMurray to Edmonton

WestJet + Air Canada · Daily flights

Edmonton is Fort McMurray's second busiest route. Many Fort Mac workers live in the Edmonton metro area — Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Leduc — making YMM-YEG one of the most important commuter routes in Northern Alberta.

Flight time: Approximately 55 minutes

Why fly YMM-YEG: Home to Edmonton and area. Also connects to direct international flights from YEG — Amsterdam, Houston, Denver, Los Angeles, Reykjavik.

Typical price range: $180-$400 CAD roundtrip

✈️ YMM → YYZ — Fort McMurray to Toronto

Air Canada · Direct · 3h 48min

The Fort McMurray to Toronto direct flight is one of the most important routes in the oil sands workforce ecosystem. A huge proportion of Fort Mac's temporary workforce comes from Atlantic Canada — Nova Scotia, Newfoundland, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island — and flies through Toronto to get home. The Air Canada direct service saves these workers from a connecting stop in Calgary or Edmonton.

Flight time: 3 hours 48 minutes direct — the longest direct flight from YMM

Who uses this route: Oil sands workers on rotation (2 weeks on, 2 weeks off) commuting to and from Atlantic Canada and Southern Ontario. High demand on Sundays (flying in for Monday starts) and Fridays/Saturdays (flying home for time off).

Typical price range: $450-$800 CAD roundtrip

WestJet · Direct

Flight time: Approximately 2 hours 10 minutes

Typical price range: $300-$600 CAD roundtrip

Flying International from Fort McMurray

Fort McMurray has no direct international flights. For international travel, you connect through Calgary or Edmonton — both under an hour away by air.

📋 Which connecting city is better for international travel? It depends on your destination. Calgary has more direct European routes. Edmonton has direct to Amsterdam and is slightly closer to Fort Mac.

Tips for Fort McMurray Travellers

🗓️ Book on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. YMM fares are highest on Sundays (workers flying in) and Thursdays/Fridays (workers flying home). Mid-week booking and mid-week travel dates are almost always cheaper.

📱 Set price alerts for your regular route. If you fly the same route every rotation, set a Google Flights alert and book when prices dip. YMM-YYZ prices can swing $200+ depending on day and demand.

🧳 Check baggage allowance before booking. If you are checking tools or equipment, confirm what is allowed. WestJet and Air Canada have different rules for oversized and overweight items common in trades work.

⏰ Allow extra time at YMM during shift changes. The airport gets busy during major rotation periods — Sunday evenings and Monday mornings especially. Arrive at least 90 minutes before departure.

Fort McMurray Airport Amenities

YMM is a mid-size regional airport with the basics covered well:

  • Food and beverage options airside and in the main terminal
  • Free WiFi throughout the terminal
  • Car rental desks (Hertz, Avis, National, Enterprise)
  • Taxi and rideshare pickup available
  • Covered and outdoor parking available
  • No airport lounge — Air Canada Maple Leaf Club members should note this
