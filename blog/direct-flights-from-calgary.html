Direct International Flights from Calgary (YYC) — Every Nonstop Route in 2026

By Aunt Tia · July 3, 2026 · 6 min read

Calgary International Airport is quietly one of the best-connected airports in Western Canada for international travel. More nonstop routes than most people realize — including direct flights to London, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Abu Dhabi, Rome, Barcelona, Dublin and Reykjavik. No Toronto connection required.

"Calgary gets overshadowed by Toronto and Vancouver for international travel. That is genuinely their loss and your gain — fewer people know about these routes, which means better prices and emptier planes."

Year-Round Direct International Routes from YYC

These routes operate every week of the year — not just in summer. If you can be flexible on dates, these are your most reliable options for direct international travel from Calgary.

✈️ YYC → LHR — Calgary to London Heathrow

Air Canada + WestJet · Year-round

Calgary has not one but TWO airlines operating direct to London year-round — Air Canada and WestJet both fly this route, which means more seat availability and competitive pricing. London Heathrow is one of the world's premier connecting airports, making YYC-LHR a gateway to virtually anywhere in Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

Flight time: Approximately 9 hours westbound, 8.5 hours eastbound.

Remember: UK ETA required since February 2026 — apply at gov.uk before flying.

Typical price range: $900-$1,400 CAD roundtrip economy · $3,000-$5,000 CAD business class

✈️ YYC → CDG — Calgary to Paris Charles de Gaulle

WestJet · Year-round

WestJet operates year-round direct service from Calgary to Paris — one of the most popular international routes out of YYC. Paris CDG is also a major hub for Air France and its partners, making it an excellent connecting point for the rest of Europe, Africa and beyond.

Flight time: Approximately 9.5 hours westbound, 9 hours eastbound.

Typical price range: $850-$1,300 CAD roundtrip economy

See current YYC → CDG deals →

✈️ YYC → AMS — Calgary to Amsterdam

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines · Year-round

KLM operates year-round direct service from Calgary to Amsterdam Schiphol. Schiphol is consistently ranked among the world's best airports for connections — clean, efficient, and with direct routes to virtually everywhere in Europe within 2-3 hours. If you want to explore multiple European countries, flying into Amsterdam and out of a different city is a very smart strategy.

Typical price range: $850-$1,250 CAD roundtrip economy

See current YYC → AMS deals →

✈️ YYC → FRA — Calgary to Frankfurt

Discover Airlines · Year-round

Discover Airlines operates year-round direct service between Calgary and Frankfurt. Frankfurt is Germany's financial capital and one of Europe's most important aviation hubs — Lufthansa's home base with connections across Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Flying into Frankfurt also puts you within easy train reach of Munich, Cologne, Berlin and most of Central Europe.

Typical price range: $900-$1,350 CAD roundtrip economy

See current YYC → FRA deals →

✈️ YYC → AUH — Calgary to Abu Dhabi

Etihad Airways · Year-round

Etihad operates year-round direct service from Calgary to Abu Dhabi — the longest nonstop flight from YYC at approximately 14 hours. Abu Dhabi is the gateway to the UAE and from there you can connect to virtually anywhere in Asia, Africa and the Indian subcontinent on Etihad's network. Abu Dhabi itself is a spectacular destination — the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque alone is worth the trip.

Flight time: Approximately 14 hours eastbound.

Note: UAE entry is visa on arrival for Canadians — free, 30 days. Read cultural guidelines before visiting.

Typical price range: $1,100-$1,800 CAD roundtrip economy · $4,000-$7,000 CAD business class

Seasonal Direct Routes from YYC

These routes operate during peak travel seasons — primarily spring and summer. Book early as seats fill up fast.

✈️ YYC → FCO — Calgary to Rome

WestJet · Seasonal (Spring/Summer)
Seasonal

WestJet operates seasonal direct service from Calgary to Rome Fiumicino in spring and summer. Direct to Rome from Calgary — no London or Frankfurt layover. Rome is one of the world's greatest cities with 2,800 years of history, the Vatican, the Colosseum, and some of the best food on Earth.

Typical price range: $950-$1,400 CAD roundtrip economy

✈️ YYC → BCN — Calgary to Barcelona

WestJet · Seasonal (Spring/Summer)
Seasonal

WestJet operates seasonal direct service from Calgary to Barcelona El Prat. Barcelona is one of Europe's most vibrant cities — Gaudi architecture, beaches, world-class food and nightlife. Flying direct from Calgary saves the typical 6-8 hours of connections most Canadians endure to reach Barcelona.

Typical price range: $900-$1,350 CAD roundtrip economy

✈️ YYC → DUB — Calgary to Dublin

WestJet · Seasonal (Summer)
Seasonal

WestJet operates seasonal summer direct service from Calgary to Dublin. Ireland is one of the most Canadian-friendly destinations in the world — a massive Irish diaspora in Canada means many Albertans have roots there. Direct from Calgary, no UK stopover needed. Ireland also does not require an ETA.

Typical price range: $850-$1,300 CAD roundtrip economy

✈️ YYC → KEF — Calgary to Reykjavik

WestJet · Seasonal (Summer)
Seasonal

WestJet operates seasonal summer direct service from Calgary to Keflavik Iceland. Iceland is extraordinary — volcanoes, geysers, Northern Lights, midnight sun, Blue Lagoon and some of the most dramatic landscapes on Earth. Direct from Calgary makes Iceland far more accessible than most Albertans realize.

Typical price range: $750-$1,150 CAD roundtrip economy

✈️ YYC → EDI — Calgary to Edinburgh

WestJet · Seasonal (Summer)
Seasonal

WestJet operates seasonal summer direct service from Calgary to Edinburgh — one of the most underrated routes out of YYC. Edinburgh is a stunning city with a castle, world-class whisky, and the Scottish Highlands on its doorstep. Direct from Calgary means no London Heathrow chaos.

Remember: UK ETA required — Edinburgh is in Scotland, which is part of the UK.

Typical price range: $850-$1,300 CAD roundtrip economy

Edmontonians — Is it Worth Driving to Calgary?

For many Edmonton residents, the answer is yes. The 3-hour drive to Calgary opens up significantly more direct international routes — especially for Europe. Here is a quick comparison:

  • From YEG direct: Amsterdam, Houston, Denver, LA, Reykjavik
  • From YYC direct: London, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Abu Dhabi, Rome, Barcelona, Dublin, Reykjavik, Edinburgh

If you want to fly direct to London, Paris, Frankfurt or Abu Dhabi, the drive to Calgary is worth it. Park at the airport and go.
