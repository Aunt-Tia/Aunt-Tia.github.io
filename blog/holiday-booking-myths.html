When to Actually Book Your Holiday Flights

By Aunt Tia · August 2026

Everyone's got a rule. "Book on a Tuesday." "Wait exactly 6 weeks." "Clear your cookies or they'll raise the price." Most of it's nonsense. Here's what actually matters, myth by myth.

Myth
"Book on a Tuesday afternoon for the best price."
Reality

This was arguably true a decade ago, back when airlines released sales on a predictable weekly cycle. That pattern is long gone — prices now update constantly, all week, based on demand. There is no magic day. Sorry.

Myth
"Incognito mode stops airlines from raising your price."
Reality

Mostly a myth. Prices shift because of real seat inventory and demand, not because a website is tracking your one search and personally punishing you. It won't hurt to use incognito, but it's not the trick people think it is.

Myth
"Prices always drop if you wait until the last minute."
Reality

True for some routes, some of the year. Almost never true for Christmas or New Year's. Holiday flights are one of the most predictable exceptions — demand is guaranteed, seats sell out, and airlines have zero incentive to discount a flight everyone already wants.

Myth
"There's one exact magic number of days before departure."
Reality

The "book exactly 54 days out" advice floating around the internet is a wildly oversimplified average, mashed together from every kind of trip imaginable. Domestic, international, holiday, off-season — they all behave differently. A single magic number was never real.

What actually works for Christmas & New Year's

Now

Start watching prices for your route. You don't have to book yet — just get a sense of what "normal" looks like before demand ramps up.

By mid-Oct

This is genuinely the sweet spot for most holiday routes. Early enough that seats and reasonable prices still exist, late enough that you're not paying a premium for booking absurdly early either.

November

Still workable, but your options start shrinking fast, especially for popular direct routes. Every week you wait, fewer good seats remain.

December

This is where the myth of "last-minute deals" goes to die. Expect the highest prices of the season, if there's anything left to book at all.
