ETIAS for Canadians — Europe's New Pre-Travel Authorization Explained

By Aunt Tia · July 3, 2026 · 5 min read

The European Union is launching a new pre-travel authorization called ETIAS — expected in late 2026. Every Canadian travelling to Europe will need one before boarding their flight. It is not a visa, it costs €7, and it takes about 10 minutes. Here is everything you need to know before it launches.

Current Status — July 2026

ETIAS is NOT yet required. The EU has confirmed a Q4 2026 launch target but no exact date has been announced. You do not need to do anything right now — but you should know what is coming. Aunt Tia will update this page the moment an official launch date is confirmed.

"Seven euros. Roughly twelve Canadian dollars. For three years of hassle-free European travel. The bureaucracy is annoying but the price is genuinely reasonable — do not let it put you off booking that Paris flight."

What is ETIAS?

ETIAS stands for European Travel Information and Authorisation System. It is a pre-travel security screening system being introduced by the European Union for visitors from countries that currently enter Europe visa-free — including Canada, the United States, the UK, Australia and many others.

Think of it as Europe's version of what Canada already does with the eTA system, or what the US does with ESTA. You apply online before your trip, pay a small fee, and receive an electronic authorization linked to your passport.

An ETIAS is NOT a visa. You do not need to attend an appointment, submit biometric data, or wait weeks. It is a digital background check that most applicants will have approved within minutes.

ETIAS Quick Facts

  • Cost: €7 per person (approximately $10-12 CAD)
  • Validity: 3 years or until your passport expires — whichever is first
  • How long per visit: Up to 90 days in any 180-day period
  • Application time: About 10 minutes online
  • Approval time: Usually within minutes, up to 4 days in some cases
  • Who needs it: All Canadians visiting Schengen Area countries
  • Under 18 or over 70: Exempt from the €7 fee
  • Launch date: Expected Q4 2026 — exact date TBC

ETIAS vs UK ETA — What is the Difference?

These are two completely separate systems run by two completely different governments:

  • UK ETA — British system. Covers England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland. Already required since February 2026. Costs £10. Valid 2 years.
  • EU ETIAS — European Union system. Covers 27 Schengen countries. Launching Q4 2026. Costs €7. Valid 3 years.

If your Europe trip combines the UK and Schengen countries — say, London then Paris — you will eventually need both. The UK ETA is required now. ETIAS is not yet required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to apply for ETIAS right now?

No. ETIAS is not yet active as of July 2026. The EU has announced a Q4 2026 launch target. You cannot apply yet. Aunt Tia will update this page and send an email to subscribers when the application opens.

I am flying to Europe in late 2026 — will I need ETIAS?

Possibly. The EU has said Q4 2026 but has not confirmed an exact date. If you are flying in October, November or December 2026, monitor the official ETIAS website and this page for updates. We will alert subscribers immediately when it launches.

I have a European trip booked — what should I do now?

Nothing yet — you cannot apply. Make a note to check this page and the official ETIAS website closer to your travel date. Set a reminder for September 2026 to check for launch news.

Will one ETIAS cover multiple European countries?

Yes. One ETIAS covers the entire Schengen Area for 3 years. You can visit France, Germany, Italy, Spain and 23 other Schengen countries on a single ETIAS without reapplying.

What if I am just transiting through a European airport?

In most cases yes, you will need an ETIAS even for transit through Schengen airports. Some exceptions may apply — check the official ETIAS site for your specific situation when it launches.
